# CommonsenseQA Single-Agent Prompt

<System Task>
Answer the following CommonsenseQA multiple-choice question independently.

Choose the single best answer from A, B, C, D, or E.

<Reasoning>
Reason briefly using common-sense knowledge and compare the answer choices.

After reasoning, provide your initial answer.

<Self-Reflection>
Briefly reconsider the question and all choices.

If you identify a concrete reasoning error or another option clearly fits better, revise your answer.
Otherwise, keep the initial answer unchanged.

<Output Format>
Reasoning: <concise reasoning>

Initial answer: <A-E>

Reflection: <brief verification>

Final answer: <A-E>

<Answer Rules>
- Initial answer and Final answer must each be exactly one uppercase letter from A to E.
- Do not include option text or explanations on the answer lines.
